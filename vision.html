GTS: Unofficial Monsterverse Product Vision Document

Problem Statement

Players who want to play the monster in a big modded Skyrim (vampire, werewolf, necromancer) spend their time fixing load-order conflicts. In a curated list like Gate to Sovngarde, the monster paths are thin: lycanthropy is a once-a-day power, and vampirism is a hunger bar. Dropped onto GTS on its own, each deeper overhaul (Everdamned, Growl, Moonlight Tales, Bloodlines of Tamriel) clashes with the scripts GTS already ships, double-applies silver and sun weaknesses, or quietly breaks a patch. Players have to choose between a thin curse that works and a deeper one that conflicts with GTS. And the world barely reacts: a witnessed transformation or feeding costs a bounty at most, and nobody hunts the beast.

Vision

Our vision is a single add-on collection that makes every monster path in Gate to Sovngarde a full playthrough, where vampires and werewolves grow stronger over a whole game and the world reacts to them. By picking one owner for each monster system, building small add-ons on top of those owners instead of more overhauls, and patching every conflict with GTS, we make sure a player can install it, start a new game and live as a vampire from first bite to Nightmaster, or as a werewolf, without opening xEdit.

Key Features

  • Vampire play: Everdamned vampirism tuned for GTS, with claws, quests and friendly vampires that don't burn in daylight fights.
  • Werewolf play: GTS's werewolf curse plus Half-Beast, a middle form with claws and an all-fours sprint. A helmet or hood keeps witnesses from recognising you.
  • Hunters: the Silver Hand come after witnessed transformations, and the Dawnguard and Vigilants after witnessed feedings.
  • The restless dead and Oblivion: Quiet's undead tweaks, Mihail's daedra and draugr, and entries for them in The Dragonborn's Bestiary.
  • Vampire bloodlines: choose your clan when you turn (Volkihar, Cyrodilic, Aundae, Berne or Quarra). Your clan opens its own branch of a bloodline skill tree built with Wiki_Ward, and finishing Dawnguard opens a second, so no vampire masters everything.
  • One install: the collection goes on top of GTS + Community Edition and keeps working through GTS updates.

Guiding Principles

  1. One owner per monster: each system (vampire, werewolf curse, sun, silver) has exactly one mod in charge. Everything else adds on top or stays out.
  2. Add-ons, not overhauls: when something is missing, we build a small add-on that hooks the owner's signals. We never replace its scripts.
  3. Lore before spectacle: every creature and power needs a named Elder Scrolls source and is placed where lore puts it. Imports from other franchises don't go in.
  4. Belong in GTS: never duplicate a mod that GTS or a companion collection already installs, and every patch is mastered on what it overrides.
  5. Credit is part of the build: nothing is redistributed without its author's permission. Other authors' work is linked, never re-uploaded.

Who It's For

  • GTS players starting a new character who want to play as a vampire or werewolf.
  • GTS players who stay mortal but want monster hunting to be dangerous and varied.
  • Wiki_Ward and other GTS add-on authors, as a shared base for monster mods that already work together.

Non-Goals

  • Creature textures stay with GTS, which already ships the replacers.
  • We leave GTS's combat stack alone. The only animations we add are monster-specific.
  • Anything that needs GTS itself changed goes to the GTS team.
  • It doesn't support existing saves: it changes how vampirism works, so it's for new games.

Success Looks Like

  • A fresh GTS + CE profile installs it with no manual xEdit work, with and without the Rot Below companion.
  • A full vampire run (turned, ranked up to Nightmaster, Vampire Lord) and a full werewolf run (cursed, Half-Beast, hunted) finish without crashes caused by the collection.
  • After every GTS or CE update, our patches still win their records before a new revision ships.
