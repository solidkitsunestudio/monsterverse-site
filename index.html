Overview: Nexus collection page (draft)

Play as the monster, or hunt it. Monsterverse is an unofficial add-on collection for JaySerpa's Gate to Sovngarde. It turns vampirism, lycanthropy and the restless dead into full playthroughs: Everdamned vampires, a werewolf curse with a middle form, Mihail's daedra and draugr, and undead that rise again. Every creature it adds has an Elder Scrolls source and turns up where lore puts it. The collection is patched to run on top of GTS and Community Edition.

What you get

Vampires

  • Everdamned replaces GTS's player vampirism: Vitae instead of a hunger bar, age ranks that grow your powers, three Vampirism trees and a reworked Vampire Lord. It runs on Wiki_Ward's GTS Scion Blend, which keeps GTS's balance and Scion's NPC side intact.
  • NPC Vampire Powers (Wiki_Ward): the vampires you fight use Everdamned's powers too.
  • Vampire Talons (Wiki_Ward): every vampire, you included, has talons on bare hands that extend in a fight. (beta)
  • Better Vampire Weapons: a clue-driven mystery across four hidden vampire lairs, with new weapons and enchantments.
  • A small patch of ours stops Serana and vampires who like you from catching fire in daylight fights.

Werewolves and Half-Beast

  • GTS's Manbeast curse, perk tree and howls stay.
  • Half-Beast (Wiki_Ward): a partial transformation with fur, claws and an all-fours sprint. You keep talking, looting and opening doors.
  • In Half-Beast form, your unarmed attacks become Feral's claw swipes. This works without BFCO, so it runs on Motion Kombat for GTS.
  • Forced Shift (beta), our own: sometimes the beast decides. With the Cornered Beast perk, a bad wound in a fight while your hunger runs high forces you into Half-Beast. With Beast Unleashed, a worse one forces the full werewolf, once a day. Master of the Beast holds it back for a day, and every threshold is a setting.

Undead

  • Quiet's Undead and Vampire Tweaks picks: grave digging and necromancy, undead that rise again, summons that claw out of the ground, ghost shaders, and silver, sun and garlic that hurt vampires. A patch of ours makes the silver armour from GTS's other mods count as silver too.
  • Draugr Patrols and Cavalry: draugr ride out at night on undead horses, with hounds, along the roads near their tombs.
  • Torch-wielder Draugrs: lesser draugr carry torches through their barrows, alongside the draugr already there.

Daedra

  • Mihail's Daedra Pack: creatures from across the planes of Oblivion, with three new areas.
  • Held to the lore: creatures with no Elder Scrolls source, the Mind Flayers among them, are switched off. A few take their lore names (the Banekamp becomes the Stonefire Scamp, as in ESO). Daedra only turn up where something in the world summons or serves them: conjurers' lairs, their Prince's shrines, vampire lairs and Apocrypha, not Nordic barrows or Falmer ruins.
  • Mihail's daedra have entries in The Dragonborn's Bestiary, under their lore names.

Hunters (beta)

  • Being seen has a price. The Silver Hand come for you after witnessed transformations, Half-Beast included, and the Dawnguard and Vigilants after witnessed feedings. In Half-Beast, a helmet or hood hides your face. Witnesses fight or run, patrols grow each time they come, and they find you on the roads, never in towns or dungeons. A settings menu shows how close they are.

Bloodlines (beta)

  • Choose your clan when you turn: Volkihar, Cyrodilic, Aundae, Cronvangr or Quarra (Harkon's gift makes you Volkihar). Cyrodilic vampires pass for mortal: the sun hurts them less, and even starving they don't draw people's hate. The Cronvangr are our own, an old Nord coven of stealth out of Cronvangr Hall in Eastmarch.
  • The Bloodline tree, built with Wiki_Ward from his VampCSF: your clan opens its own branch, and finishing Dawnguard opens a second, so no vampire masters everything. Feeding grows it, its powers cost Vitae, and each capstone needs the matching Everdamned mastery. Volkihar's branch is Frost and Grave: freeze your prey, raise the dead. Everdamned itself is never changed.
  • A vampire's eyes: Cyrodilic vampires, and any vampire with Masquerade, keep mortal eyes while fed, and hunger turns them red. Once you take a bloodline capstone, the red stays (Masquerade still hides it). Pick from eighteen red eye styles; the glow in a fight is red, not orange.
  • Unmask: drop the mortal disguise and show the monster: the face, the snarl, the fangs and the eyes, at a cost of Vitae every second. Starving and badly hurt, the mask can tear away on its own. Unmask where someone can see your face and it counts as a witnessed feeding; a hood hides it.

Requirements

  • Gate to Sovngarde and GTS Community Edition, installed, launched and working on this profile first.
  • The Skyrim version GTS asks for. Don't update or downgrade Skyrim separately.
  • Extra disk space: to be measured at release.

Installation

  1. Install Gate to Sovngarde and Community Edition, launch once, and make sure the game works.
  2. Install Monsterverse on the same profile.
  3. Run Pandora after install and save its output as its own mod (instructions in the collection's mod notes). Everdamned adds animations and behaviours, so GTS's pre-built behaviour output has to be regenerated.
  4. Start a new game. Monsterverse replaces how vampirism works, so it isn't safe to add to a save that already has a vampire or werewolf character.
Important

Some mods are deliberately switched off because they conflict with Everdamned (Just Bite - Scion, Scion DAK, Just Bite - GTS Patch). Leave them off.

Recommended companion collection

  • GTS Unofficial Addon: Rot Below by Ultimersiv. The Rot Below: a mad lich, Namira's cult and three boss fights, for level 30+. It's been tested alongside Monsterverse (confirm in release testing). Monsterverse doesn't list its mods, so you won't get duplicates. Install it on the same profile if you want it.

Compatibility

  • Built for GTS v116 + Community Edition r91 (update at release).
  • Other GTS add-on collections: fine unless they also replace player vampirism or the werewolf curse.
  • Not compatible with other vampire overhauls (Sacrosanct, Bloodlines of Tamriel, Better Vampires) or werewolf overhauls (Growl, Moonlight Tales, Wolves of Nirn). Monsterverse already owns those systems.
